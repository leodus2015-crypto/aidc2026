07 — Planning MethodAI Data Centers

AI DC planning methodology: Token → Card → Power

One causal chain. Size compute from the business workload, then size power, cooling and floor area from the cards. Each stage has an explicit formula and can be solved forward or reverse.

01 · Token

Business workload

Start from users, penetration and usage. Convert that into daily tokens Td and peak throughput Ts.

Watch: tokens per user, cache hit rate, and the mix of inference scenarios.

Td = users × tokens/user · Ts = Td ÷ 86,400

02 · Card

Accelerator count

Convert Ts into theoretical FLOPS, then into NPU count with margin. Check the HBM memory floor.

Watch: FLOPS per token, utilization, redundancy, and whether memory—not compute—sets the floor.

ncard = max( ⌈Ctheory × K ÷ Ccard⌉ , memory floor )

03 · Power

Power, cooling, space

Convert cards into racks, IT design power, room power and floor area. Cooling choice changes density.

Watch: cards per rack, rack kW, PUE, and air versus liquid cooling.

PIT = nrack × Prack · Proom = PIT × PUE

Worked example · not a target

Same chain, one 1,024-card liquid-cooled supernode

40.6Btokens / day · 470k token/s · 1,908 AI-active users
1,024cards · 128 servers × 8 · 940.8 PFLOPS with margin
2.10 MWIT design · 2.41 MW room · 206 m² · 40 racks in 2 pods

On the same method, a 768-card air-cooled node needs 2.50 MW and 404 m² — more cards do not automatically mean more power or space.

Source: AIDC 2026, Token → Card → Power methodology. Example figures illustrate the chain; they are not a recommended deployment size.
